Guvenli QR: QR Kod Kontrol - Privacy Policy

Developer: KALEM Apps · Package: com.kalemapps.guvenliqr · Last updated: 12 October 2026

Summary

Guvenli QR does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no user accounts.

Camera permission

The app asks for the camera permission only to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera images are processed on your device in real time and are never saved, uploaded or shared.

Scanned codes

The content of the codes you scan is shown to you on the screen. The app can run simple safety checks on a scanned link locally on your device, and it can keep a scan history on your device so you can review past scans. This information stays on your device only: it is never sent to our servers, never shared with any third party, and it is removed when you uninstall the app. The app never opens a link on its own; you decide whether to open it.

Data collection and sharing

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not share data with third parties.
  • The app does not request internet access for its own features.
  • The app does not show ads and does not contain trackers.

Third-party components

The app uses Google ML Kit barcode scanning, which runs on your device. Google Play services may process limited technical diagnostics as described in Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The app is a general utility and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.

Guvenli QR: QR Kod Kontrol - Gizlilik Politikasi

Gelistirici: KALEM Apps · Paket: com.kalemapps.guvenliqr · Son guncelleme: 12 Ekim 2026

Ozet

Guvenli QR hicbir kisisel veriyi toplamaz, saklamaz, paylasmaz veya satmaz. Uygulamada reklam, analiz araci ve kullanici hesabi yoktur.

Kamera izni

Uygulama kamera iznini yalnizca QR kod ve barkod taramak icin ister. Kamera goruntusu cihazinizda anlik olarak islenir; kaydedilmez, yuklenmez ve paylasilmaz.

Taranan kodlar

Taradiginiz kodun icerigi size ekranda gosterilir. Uygulama, taranan baglanti uzerinde cihazinizda basit guvenlik kontrolleri yapabilir ve gecmis taramalarinizi gorebilmeniz icin tarama gecmisini cihazinizda tutabilir. Bu bilgiler yalnizca cihazinizda kalir: sunucularimiza gonderilmez, ucuncu taraflarla paylasilmaz ve uygulama silindiginde kaldirilir. Uygulama hicbir baglantiyi kendiliginden acmaz; acip acmamaya siz karar verirsiniz.

Veri toplama ve paylasim

  • Kisisel bilgi toplamayiz.
  • Verileri ucuncu taraflarla paylasmayiz.
  • Uygulama kendi ozellikleri icin internet erisimi istemez.
  • Uygulamada reklam ve izleyici (tracker) yoktur.

Ucuncu taraf bilesenler

Uygulama, cihaz uzerinde calisan Google ML Kit barkod tarama bilesenini kullanir. Google Play hizmetleri, Google'in kendi gizlilik politikasinda aciklandigi sekilde sinirli teknik tani verisi isleyebilir: policies.google.com/privacy.

Cocuklar

Uygulama genel amacli bir aractir ve cocuklar dahil hicbir kisiden bilerek bilgi toplamaz.

Degisiklikler

Bu politika degisirse yeni surum ayni adreste guncel tarihle yayinlanir.
